# Job Radar

Job Radar collects job openings straight from company career sites and the main job boards, so you see new
jobs first, in one list, filtered your way. Free while it is being improved with feedback from its first users.

- Jobs from **140+ companies'** own career sites, plus any company you add by pasting its careers page
- **Job boards** for each country you pick: some read automatically, the rest open as ready-made searches
- **Work type and office days**, **salary** (from the ad or a market estimate), **languages needed**
- **"English is enough"** filter, and roles recognised in Czech, Slovak, Polish, German, Dutch and Hungarian
- Everything stays on your computer; updates install with one click

## Download

1. Open **[Releases](../../releases/latest)** and download `Job-Radar-<version>-setup.zip`.
2. Unzip it. You get a folder called `job-radar`.

## Install on a Mac

Nothing else to install first: Job Radar brings its own copy of Python (about 25 MB, downloaded once).

1. Open **Terminal** (press Cmd + Space, type Terminal, press Enter).
2. Type `bash ` (with a space after it), drag the file `install_mac.sh` from the `job-radar` folder into the
   Terminal window, and press Enter.
3. Answer **Y** if you want a daily check for new jobs while the app is closed.

Job Radar opens and appears in **Applications**. Drag it to the Dock to keep it there.

## Install on Windows

Nothing else to install first: Job Radar brings its own copy of Python (about 48 MB, downloaded once).

1. Double-click `install_windows.bat` in the `job-radar` folder.
2. If Windows warns about the file, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Job Radar appears on the Desktop and in the Start menu.

## Updates

Job Radar checks for new versions once a day. When one is out, an **Update** button appears at the top: one
click, about a minute. Your jobs, marks and settings stay.

## Feedback

Use the **Feedback** button at the top of the app. Every message is read. After 2 and 4 weeks the app asks 3
short questions; your answers decide what gets built next.

## Privacy

Job Radar runs on your computer. Anonymous usage data is shared only if you agree on first start, and never
includes your name, CV, searches or the jobs you look at. Details: [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).
