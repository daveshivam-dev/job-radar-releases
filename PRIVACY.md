# Job Radar privacy note

Job Radar runs on your computer. Your saved jobs, marks, settings and search words stay there.

## What leaves your computer

**Always, because the app needs it:** requests to the company career sites and job boards you search, and a
daily check for new versions of Job Radar on GitHub. These sites see an ordinary request from your internet
connection, as when you visit them in a browser.

**Only if you agree** (first start, or Settings > Help improve Job Radar; you can change it at any time):

- that this copy was used on a day, its version, and the computer's system (for example "macOS 15.1")
- the countries, languages, role types and job boards you picked, and how many companies are in your list
- how often parts of the app are used (for example "the Language filter was used 3 times"), never what you
  typed or which jobs you looked at
- how many jobs you saved or marked as applied, as numbers only
- which company feeds or job boards could not be read, and careers pages that could not be added
- error reports, with your user name and email addresses removed

**Only when you send it yourself:** the feedback form and the short survey (after 2 and 4 weeks). Add an email
address only if you want an answer.

## What is never sent

Your name, CV, the job titles or words you search for, which jobs you saved, opened or applied to, or anything
from the job ads you read.

## Where it goes

To PostHog's EU cloud in Frankfurt, Germany, under a random ID made for this copy of the app (Settings shows
it). The ID is not linked to your name, and the service is set not to keep your internet address. Data is kept
for up to 1 year. To have it deleted, send feedback with your ID.
